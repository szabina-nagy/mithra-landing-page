Scams have become part of everyday digital life.

Fraud increasingly reaches people through familiar online services, messages and phone calls. Scammers often imitate trusted organisations and create urgency, making it difficult to pause and judge whether something is genuine.

Learn to recognise common tactics

Scams often use the same psychological tactics. Recognising them can make suspicious messages easier to identify.

Urgency

Pressure to respond immediately or risk a negative consequence.

Unexpected payments

Requests for unusual fees, refunds or unfamiliar payment methods.

Suspicious links

The displayed name may look familiar while the real address is different.
